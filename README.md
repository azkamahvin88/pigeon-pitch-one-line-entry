A make.fun entry for **Pigeon Pitch, One Line**: one funny line — 168 characters — a city pigeon's elevator pitch to investors for its startup. The program writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Investors, my startup does targeted advertising: for a monthly fee, a thousand pigeons will only poop on your competitor's headquarters. We call it precision marketing.

The joke is a literalisation: "targeted advertising" turns out to mean pigeons aiming, and the kicker "precision marketing" lands the same joke a second time, deadpan, in the last clause. The pitch opens with "Investors,", names the business in the first clause, and could only come from a pigeon — a thousand birds, a competitor's headquarters, aiming from above. It was picked from 12 candidates scored against the judge brief's five criteria (on-subject pitch first, a real joke that lands, originality, pigeon voice, tight fit), and it steers clear of the field's saturated breadcrumb / seed-round / crumb-subscription angle entirely.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
